# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Owners (primary surface).** One owner per business, working standing up and exclusively from a phone, between one attention and the next. Reference cases: Marina, who runs a hair salon with individual appointments, two workers, and four stations, and Lucía, who runs a yoga studio with recurring group classes, fixed monthly memberships, and drop-in students. Today they run the business on a notebook, loose pages, and spreadsheets, and coordinate everything through WhatsApp. Workers and teachers are data, not users.

**Customers (public booking).** People who book, confirm, cancel, or reschedule from a shared link without creating an account. About a third of a salon's customers are over 65; if booking is not simple and large, they keep writing on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

WorkUp replaces the counter notebook for appointment-based service businesses: agenda, weekly class grid, memberships, attendance, charges, walk-ins, expenses, declared stock, automatic reminders, and period results. Success means the owner records reality faster than with a pen, customers book on their own outside business hours, and nothing is lost or duplicated. Source of truth for scope: `srs.md`.

## Brand Personality

**Trade tool.** Direct, sturdy, dependable. It should feel like a well-made working instrument, such as good shears or a cash register: obviously built to work fast, with no ornament. Confidence comes from clarity and speed, not from decoration or friendliness theater. Copy is short, plain Argentine Spanish.

## Anti-references

- **"Warm AI" look:** cream backgrounds, sage green, rounded pills everywhere, soft stacked cards, uppercase tracked micro-labels. This is what the first generation in Stitch produced; do not return to it.
- **Wellness / spa apps:** pastels, candles and leaves imagery, thin "zen" typefaces.
- **Legacy management systems:** dense grey tables, Windows-style menus, endless forms.

## Design Principles

1. **Faster than the pen.** Recording a charge, an attendance, or a walk-in takes fewer taps than writing it in the notebook. If not, owners go back to paper.
2. **The tool does not argue.** It shows conflicts but lets the owner record what already happened ("si yo lo hice, lo hice"). Validation blocks only reservations.
3. **Useful density, no decoration.** The day reads at a glance: time, person, state. No filler cards, showcase metrics, or ornament.
4. **State in plain words.** Every state uses the closed glossary term (`Pendiente`, `Confirmado`, `Atendido`, `Cobrado`, ...) and never relies on color alone.
5. **Public means anyone.** A 70-year-old customer must be able to book, confirm, or cancel without help.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum on every surface; mandatory on public booking (SRS non-functional requirement): 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text, zoom to 200% without loss, full keyboard operation, screen-reader compatibility.
- Older customers: large base text, large touch targets, one decision per step, no motion-dependent interaction.
- Owners use the app one-handed and under counter lighting: high contrast, thumb-reachable primary actions.
- Color-blind safe: state always carries a text label and an icon.
- Respect reduced motion. Owner-side high-contrast and larger-text preferences.
